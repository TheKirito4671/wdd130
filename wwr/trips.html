Featured Trip Highlights

Thunder Gorge Expedition

A high-adrenaline, full-day excursion through challenging Class IV rapids. Ideal for experienced rafters seeking heavy splashes, massive drops, and unforgettable canyon walls.

Canyon Family Float

A gentle, scenic half-day river journey designed specifically for families and nature lovers. Enjoy calm stretches, wildlife viewing, and relaxing floating moments.

Sunset Canyon Run

Experience the river as evening sets in. This intermediate twilight trip combines moderate Class II-III rapids with stunning golden hour views across the canyon cliffs.
